Suppose two distinct points $x,y \in X$ that are topologically indistinguishable, take $U\ni x$ open such that $U\setminus \{x\}$ is open. 
Then $y\in U$ and $y\in U \setminus \{x\}$, which is an open set containing $y$ but not $x$, which contradicts indistinguishability. Thus $X$ is {P1}.
