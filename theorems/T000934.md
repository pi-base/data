Let $x\in X$, by {P90} there exists a minimal open neighborhood $U_x$ of $x$.
Now consider $W=\bigcup\{U_y : y \in U_x, y\neq x\}$, which is open in $X$. 

We claim that $U_x \setminus \{x\} = W$. 

For $\subseteq$: each $y$ lies in $U_y$ which is in $W$. 

For $\supseteq$: if $y \in U_x$ and $x\neq y$, then $U_y \subseteq U_x$, and $x \notin U_y$ since that would contradict minimality of $U_x$ and that by {P1} such minimal open sets are unique to each point.

Hence $U_x \setminus \{x\}$ is open which shows {P247}.
