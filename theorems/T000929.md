$X$ is {P1} since {T174}.
Let $\le$ denote the corresponding specialization order ($x\le y \iff x\in\overline{\{y\}})$).
Given a point $a\in X$, its smallest neighborhood is ${\uparrow}a:=\{x\in X:x\ge a\}$.

Assume by contradiction that $X$ is not {P51}.
There is a nonempty set $A\subseteq X$ such that the subspace $A$ has no isolated point;
hence for each $a\in A$ we have ${\uparrow} a\cap A\supsetneq\{a\}$, that is, there is some $b\in A$ with $a<b$.
This allows to construct an increasing sequence $x_1<x_2<\cdots$ in $X$.

The set $B:=\{x\in X:x\le x_i \text{ for some }i\}$ is closed in $X$ and is irreducible ({P39})
(given $x,x'\in B$, their respective neighborhoods ${\uparrow}x$ and ${\uparrow}x'$ intersect).
But $B$ has no maximum point, i.e., $B$ has no generic point.
This shows that $X$ is not {P73}.
