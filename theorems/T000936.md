Let $x \in X$ and $C=\overline{\{x\}}\neq\emptyset$. 
By {P51} some $y\in C$ is isolated, so $U\cap C=\{y\}$ with $U$ open. 
Thus $U$ meets $\overline{\{x\}}$, so by openness $U$ meets $\{x\}$. 

So $x \in U\cap C = \{y\}$, hence $x=y$ and $U\cap\overline{\{x\}}=\{x\}$, giving {P247}.
