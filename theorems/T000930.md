Let $\mathcal{C}$ be the collection of finite open subsets of $X$. It is nonempty since $\emptyset \in \mathcal{C}$, so by {P208} it has a maximal element $U$.

BWOC assume $U \neq X$. Then $X \setminus U$ is a nonempty subspace, so by {P51} it contains a point $p$ isolated in it, i.e. there is an open $V \subseteq X$ with $V \cap (X \setminus U) = \{ p \}$. Then $U \cup V = U \cup \{ p \}$ is open and finite, and it strictly contains $U$, contradicting the maximality of $U$.

Hence $X = U$ is finite.
