Suppose by contradiction that $X$ is not {P46},
that is, there is a non-constant path $f:[0,1]\to X$.
Its range $f([0,1])$ is {P37} and {P100}, hence {P95}
[(Explore)](https://topology.pi-base.org/spaces?q=Path+connected%2BKC%2B%7EArc+connected).
So there is a homeomorphic embedding $g:[0,1]\to X$;
its range $g([0,1])$ is compact, hence closed in $X$ by {P100},
and hence also {P68}.

This implies that {S158} is {P68}.
But this is not the case, by a simple measure argument as used for example in {{mathse:740616}}.
