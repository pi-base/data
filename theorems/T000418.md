Let $A$ be an arbitrary subset of $X$.
By {P81}, every point $p\in\overline A$ is in the closure of a countable subset $D\subseteq A$.
One characterization of {P168} is that every countable set is closed.
So $D$ is closed and $p\in D$, which shows that $A$ is closed.
