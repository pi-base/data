The set $\omega_1$ with topology equal to the join (in the [lattice of topologies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lattice_of_topologies) on $\omega_1$) of the cofinite topology and {S217},
i.e., the topology generated by the union of these two topologies.

A base for the topology is given by the sets $\alpha\setminus F=[0,\alpha)\setminus F$ with $\alpha<\omega_1$ and $F\subseteq\omega_1$ finite.
