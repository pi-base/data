The sets $A\cup \{\omega\}$ where $A\subseteq\omega$ is cofinite form a neighbourhood base for $\omega$ and $\overline{A\cup \{\omega\}} = A\cup [\omega, \omega_1)$, which is not compact since the cover by the open sets $[0, \alpha)$ for $\alpha < \omega_1$ does not have a finite, or even countable subcover.
