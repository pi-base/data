Take some $c\in E$.
The projection map $(x,y)\mapsto y$ maps $L(c)$ homeomorphically onto $[0,\frac12]$
and maps $X\cap L(c)$, which is a closed subset of $X$, homeomorphically onto $Y:=[0,\frac12]\cap\mathbb Q$.

Now $Y$ contains a closed set homeomorphic to {S27}.
For example, choose irrational numbers $\alpha,\beta$ with $0<\alpha<\beta<\frac12$.
The set $(\alpha,\beta)\cap\mathbb Q$ is closed in $Y$;
and it is countable, dense in $(\alpha,\beta)$ and without maximum or minimum element,
hence order-isomorphic and homeomorphic to $\mathbb Q$.

It follows that $X$ contains a closed set homeomorphic to {S27}.
Since {S27|P55}, neither is $X$.
