Let $c\in E$.
The set $X\cap L(c)=\{(x,y)\in L(c): y\in\mathbb Q\}$, which is closed in $X$, is homeomorphic to $\mathbb{Q}\cap [0, 1/2]$ and so
is countably infinite, metrizable and without isolated point,
hence homeomorphic to {S27} (see characterization by Sierpinski).
And {S27|P55}.
