    name: Complement of any dense countable subset of reals is homeomorphic to irrationals
---

Take some $c\in F$.
The projection map $(x,y)\mapsto y$ maps $L(c)$ homeomorphically onto $[0,\frac12]$
and maps $X\cap L(c)$, which is a closed subset of $X$, homeomorphically onto $Y:=[0,\frac12]\setminus\mathbb Q=(0,\frac12)\setminus\mathbb Q$.
Since $(0,\frac12)$ is order-isomorphic to $\mathbb R$ and
the complement of $Y$ in $(0,\frac12)$ is countable and dense in $(0,\frac12)$,
$Y$ is homeomorphic to {S28}, as shown in {{mathse:4933189}}.

Hence $X$ contains a closed set homeomorphic to {S28}.
Since {S28|P66}, neither is $X$.
