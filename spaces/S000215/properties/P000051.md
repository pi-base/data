If $Y\subseteq X$ is non-empty then either $Y$ contains a point $(x, y)$ with $y\neq 0$, so that $(x, y)$ is isolated in $Y$,
or $Y\subseteq \mathbb{R}\times \{0\}$ and, since $U_n(x)\cap (\mathbb{R}\times \{0\}) = \{(x, 0)\}$, it follows that $Y$ is discrete.
