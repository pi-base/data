Suppose $U\subseteq X$ is a clopen set and $(0,0)\in U$.
Since each $A_n$ is {P36}, it has to be contained either in $U$ or $X\setminus U$.
Observe that all but finitely many *spokes* $A_n$ intersect $U$ (a neighbourhood of $(0,0)$), therefore they are contained in $U$. Then $U$ is a dense subset of $X$ (it is dense in the unit disc) and consequently $U=X$. Hence the space $X$ does not admit nontrivial
clopen set and is {P36}.
