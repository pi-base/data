The set $A=\{f\in \omega^{\mathfrak{c}}: |f^{-1}(\omega\setminus\{0\})|<\aleph_0\}$ is dense in $X$.
For any countable subset $D\subset A$ there exists a countable subset $J\subset \mathfrak{c}$ such that
for any $j\in \mathfrak{c}\setminus J$ and $f\in D$ we have $f(j)=0$. Therefore supoport of any $p\in\overline D$ is at most countable, while $\overline A = X$ contains points with
uncountable support.
