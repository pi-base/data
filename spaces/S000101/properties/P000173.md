The subset $A=\{f\in X: |\mathfrak{c}\setminus f^{-1}(0)|<\aleph_0 \}$ (all functions in $X$ with finite supports) is dense in $X$ by definition of the product topology.
If $X$ is {P173} then every point of $X$ is the limit of a transfinite sequence in $A$.
Consider the constant function $f\equiv 1 \in X=\overline{A}$ and suppose $(f_\alpha)_{\alpha<\lambda}\subseteq A$ converges to $f$.
By the pointwise convergence, for each $j\in\mathfrak c$ there exists $\alpha_j=\sup\{\alpha<\lambda: f_\alpha(j)\neq 1\}<\lambda$. For each $j_0\in\mathfrak c$ the set $\{j\in\mathfrak c: \alpha_j \leq \alpha_{j_0}\}$ is finite as a subset of $f_{\alpha_{j_0}+1}^{-1}(1)$. As a consequence we obtain that $\{\alpha_j: j\in\mathfrak c\}$ is countable. On the other hand this means that some $\alpha_{j_0}$ the set $\{j\in\mathfrak c:\alpha_j=\alpha_{j_0}\}$ is infinite (uncountable) which contradicts finiteness of the support of $f_{\alpha_{j_0}+1}$.
