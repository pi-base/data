The connected components of $X$ are the two lines $L_1$ and $L_2$ and the rectangles $R_n$.
The lines $L_1$ and $L_2$ belong to the same quasicomponent.
Indeed, a clopen set $U$ containing $L_1$ intersects all the $R_n$ for $n$ sufficiently large,
hence contains $R_n$ for those $n$.
But every point of $L_2$ is an accumulation point of the union of those $R_n$.
Since $U$ is closed, $L_2\subseteq U$.
