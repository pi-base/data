Proposition 2 of {{doi:10.4064/FM-97-2-53-55}} constructs a locally countable
subspace of $X$ which is not $\sigma$-discrete. By Theorem 13.3 of
{{zb:0593.28016}}, every locally countable subspace of
a weakly $\delta\theta$-refinable space is $\sigma$-discrete. Hence $X$ is not
weakly $\delta\theta$-refinable and, in particular, is not meta-Lindelöf.
