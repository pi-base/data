The collection $\{\xi^\omega : \xi < \omega_1\}$ is an open cover of $X$.
Every countable subcollection has union contained in $\xi^\omega$ for some
$\xi < \omega_1$, and $\xi^\omega$ is a proper closed subset of $X$. Thus,
no countable subcollection has dense union.
