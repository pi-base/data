A space $X$ such that for every compact Hausdorff space $K$ and every continuous map $f:K\to X$, the image $f(K)$ is closed in $X$.

Originally defined on p. 275 of {{zb:0193.23604}}.
See also <https://ncatlab.org/nlab/show/weakly+Hausdorff+topological+space>.

----
#### Meta-properties

- This property is hereditary.
- This property is preserved by arbitrary products (see Lemma 2 in {{mathse:964546}}).
