The derived set of every subset of $X$ is closed.

Equivalently:
- for every $x$ there is an open neighborhood $U$ of $x$ s.t. $U\setminus \{x\}$ is also open.
- every point is isolated in its own closure, i.e. for every $x$ there is an open neighborhood $U$ s.t. $U\cap\overline{\{x\}}=\{x\}$

----
#### Meta-properties

- This property is hereditary.
