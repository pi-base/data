---
uid: P000251
name: Cardinality $\lt 2^\mathfrak c$
aliases:
  - Cardinality $\lt\beth_2$
---

$|X| < 2^\mathfrak c=2^{2^{\aleph_0}}=\beth_2$.

That is, $X$ has smaller cardinality than $\mathcal P(\mathbb R)$, the power set of $\mathbb R$.

----
#### Meta-properties

- This property is hereditary.
